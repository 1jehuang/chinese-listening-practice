How to spot the right one

  • Noto Sans SC / Microsoft YaHei / PingFang SC feel clean and modern, with even strokes.
  • Noto Serif SC / SimSun / Songti SC feel more textbook or print-like, with serif details.
  • KaiTi looks brush-like and calligraphic, common in educational materials.
  • SimHei / Heiti SC look heavier and blockier.
