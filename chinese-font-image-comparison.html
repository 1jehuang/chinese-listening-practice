How to compare them
  • If your quiz looks clean and modern, compare Noto Sans SC and Microsoft YaHei first.
  • If it looks book-like, compare Noto Serif SC and FangSong.
  • If it looks brushy or schoolbook-like, compare the KaiTi cards.
  • If it looks thick and square, compare SimHei.
